The People Behind the Pepper


We’re a small team of chili fans who one day realized we spend more time talking about chili than doing anything else. So we decided to make it official: if it burns, we like it. And if it tastes incredible and burns… well, that’s basically our love language.


Chillizone is built on everything we enjoy the most — fresh chilis straight from the plant, honest hot sauces, strangely addictive chili chocolate, and grow kits for anyone brave enough to cultivate their own fire at home.


But the story goes back a bit further. According to family legend (and a couple of very old, very confident relatives), our great-great-great-grandfather was the first person in the village to grow chilis in his garden. Back then, people thought “spicy” meant adding one extra onion. He changed that. He shared his chili harvest with the entire village, and suddenly everyone discovered heat. Out of that fiery awakening came the now-famous spicy goulash — a dish so beloved in the Eastworld that some people claim it cures bad moods and possibly even small existential crises.


This tradition of growing, sharing, and occasionally shocking people with unexpected heat has traveled through generations until it landed in our hands. And now we’re bringing it to you.


We’re here to keep the fire alive — with good humor, good flavor, and chilis that would make our ancestor proud (or scared, depending on the variety).


Welcome to Chillizone. Taste the color, feel the fire, and enjoy the ride. 🌶️
